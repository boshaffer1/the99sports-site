OUR STORY

The 99 Network was born from real experience, not theory. Trace lived the athlete journey, navigated NIL firsthand, and spent years helping teammates secure deals, negotiate opportunities, and build their brands the right way. On the other side, he worked directly with brands, understanding what they truly value, what drives revenue, and what actually makes partnerships work. Bringing those worlds together, he created The 99 Network as a place where athletes get education, structure, and powerful AI tools that handle the complicated NIL details for them, so they can stay focused on academics and athletics.
