Do Not Sell My Information

Exercise your right to opt out of the sale of personal information

Your Privacy Rights: Under California law (CCPA), you have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We respect your privacy and make it easy to exercise this right.

What This Means

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives California residents the right to opt out of the "sale" of their personal information. Under the CCPA, "sale" has a broad definition that may include sharing information for advertising purposes.

Our Current Practices

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. However, like many websites, we may share certain information with partners for advertising and analytics purposes, which could be considered a "sale" under the broad CCPA definition.

What Information Might Be Shared

  • Website usage data and analytics
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and location data
  • Advertising identifiers and cookies
  • Demographic and interest information

How to Opt Out

You can exercise your right to opt out in the following ways:

Option 1: Online Form (Recommended)

Use the form below to submit your opt-out request. This is the fastest way to process your request.

Option 2: Email

Send an email to privacy@the99sports.com with "Do Not Sell Request" in the subject line.

Option 3: Contact Form

Use our general contact form and specify that you want to opt out of the sale of your information.

What Happens Next

Processing Time

We will process your opt-out request within:

  • Immediate: Automated opt-out for most advertising
  • 48 hours: Manual review and additional opt-outs
  • 15 days: Complete processing (as required by law)

Confirmation

You will receive an email confirmation once we have processed your request. This email will include:

  • Confirmation that your request has been processed
  • Details about what actions we took
  • Information about how to contact us with questions

Future Data Collection

Your opt-out applies to future sale of your information. We may still:

  • Collect information necessary to provide our services
  • Use information for internal business purposes
  • Share information as required by law
  • Share information with service providers (not considered a sale)

Global Privacy Controls

If your browser supports Global Privacy Control (GPC), we will automatically honor this signal as an opt-out request. No additional action is needed if you have GPC enabled.

Enabling GPC

GPC is available in some browsers and browser extensions. Check your browser settings or privacy extensions to see if this feature is available.

Other Privacy Rights

In addition to the right to opt out of sales, you may have other privacy rights under applicable laws:

  • Right to Know: Request information about how we collect and use your data
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: You cannot be discriminated against for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, please visit our Privacy Policy or contact us using the information below.
