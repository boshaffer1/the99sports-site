Refund Policy

Our commitment to fair and transparent refund practices

Last updated: January 28, 2025

Our Commitment: We strive to deliver exceptional NIL consulting services. If you're not satisfied with our services, we offer refunds under specific conditions outlined below.

1. General Refund Policy

At The 99 Sports, we are committed to providing high-quality NIL consulting services. We understand that sometimes services may not meet your expectations, and we have established this refund policy to ensure fair treatment for all clients.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be available in the following circumstances:

  • Services were not delivered as promised in the service agreement
  • Technical issues prevented access to paid services
  • Services were cancelled within the specified cancellation window
  • Duplicate charges or billing errors

2. Service-Specific Refund Terms

Service Type Refund Window Refund Amount
Consultation Services 48 hours before scheduled session 100% refund
Monthly Packages 7 days from start date Prorated refund
Educational Resources 14 days from purchase Full refund if not accessed
Platform Access 30 days from signup Prorated refund
Custom Projects Varies by agreement As specified in contract

3. Refund Process

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our support team within the eligible refund window
  • Provide your order number and reason for the refund request
  • Include any relevant documentation or details
  • Allow up to 5 business days for review and response

Refund Processing Time

Once approved, refunds will be processed as follows:

  • Credit Card: 5-10 business days
  • Bank Transfer: 3-7 business days
  • Digital Wallet: 1-3 business days

Refund Method

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. If the original payment method is unavailable, we will work with you to find an alternative solution.

4. Non-Refundable Services

The following services are generally not eligible for refunds:

  • Completed consultation sessions
  • Services delivered as agreed upon
  • Third-party fees (processing, legal, etc.)
  • Custom work that has been completed
  • Services cancelled outside the refund window
  • Educational materials that have been accessed or downloaded

5. Partial Refunds

In some cases, partial refunds may be appropriate:

Prorated Refunds

  • Monthly or annual services cancelled mid-term
  • Unused portions of service packages
  • Services partially delivered due to circumstances beyond our control

Calculation Method

Prorated refunds are calculated based on:

  • Time remaining in the service period
  • Services not yet delivered or accessed
  • Applicable fees and costs already incurred

6. Special Circumstances

Emergency Cancellations

We understand that emergencies happen. In cases of documented emergencies (medical, family, etc.), we may offer refunds outside our normal policy at our discretion.

Technical Issues

If technical problems on our end prevent you from accessing paid services, we will:

  • Resolve the issue as quickly as possible
  • Extend your service period to compensate for downtime
  • Offer a partial or full refund if issues cannot be resolved

Service Changes

If we significantly change or discontinue a service you've paid for, you may be eligible for a refund or credit toward alternative services.

7. Dispute Resolution

Internal Review

If your refund request is denied, you may request a review by our management team. Please provide additional context or documentation to support your request.

Escalation Process

  • Initial request reviewed by customer service
  • Escalation to management for complex cases
  • Final decision communicated within 10 business days

External Dispute Resolution

If you remain unsatisfied after our internal review, you may pursue dispute resolution through your payment provider or as outlined in our Terms & Conditions.

8. Contact for Refund Requests

To initiate a refund request or ask questions about our refund policy:

Refund Support

Email: refunds@the99sports.com

Subject Line: Include "Refund Request" and your order number

Response Time: We respond to refund requests within 24 hours

General Contact: Contact Form

9. Policy Updates

We may update this refund policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Significant changes will be communicated via:

  • Email notification to existing customers
  • Website announcement
  • Updated "Last Updated" date on this page

Changes will not affect refund requests made before the policy update.

Questions? Our customer service team is here to help. We're committed to resolving any issues and ensuring your satisfaction with our services. Please don't hesitate to reach out with any refund-related questions.
