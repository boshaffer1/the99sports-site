About Targeted Advertising

Targeted advertising uses information about your interests, browsing behavior, and demographics to show you more relevant ads. This can include:

  • Ads based on websites you've visited
  • Products or services related to your interests
  • Location-based promotions and offers
  • Demographic-targeted content
  • Remarketing from previous site visits

Benefits of Targeted Ads

  • More relevant and useful advertisements
  • Discovery of products and services you might be interested in
  • Reduced irrelevant advertising content
  • Support for free content and services

Your Privacy

We respect your privacy and give you control over your advertising preferences. You can opt out of targeted advertising while still seeing generic ads, or customize which types of targeting you're comfortable with.

Browser and Device Controls

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies and tracking:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Tracking prevention

Mobile Device Settings

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalized Ads
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Do Not Track

You can enable "Do Not Track" in your browser settings. While not all websites honor this setting, we respect Do Not Track signals where technically feasible.

What Happens When You Opt Out

If You Disable Targeted Ads

  • You'll still see advertisements, but they won't be personalized
  • Ads may be less relevant to your interests
  • You might see the same ad multiple times
  • Some website features may be affected

If You Keep Targeted Ads Enabled

  • Ads will be more relevant to your interests
  • Better discovery of products and services you might like
  • Improved overall browsing experience
  • Support for free content through relevant advertising

Data Used for Targeting

When you have targeting enabled, we may use the following types of information:

Website Activity

  • Pages you visit on our site
  • Time spent on different sections
  • Actions taken (form submissions, downloads, etc.)
  • Search terms used on our site

Device and Browser Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser type and version
  • Screen resolution and settings
  • Language preferences

General Location

  • Country and region (from IP address)
  • General location for local business ads
  • Time zone information

We do not use sensitive personal information for ad targeting, including information about health, finances, religion, or political beliefs.
